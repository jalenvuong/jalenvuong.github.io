**Simple PID Control for RC Car:**

- Used PID controls to keep the RC car moving in a straight line and counteract drift
- Set a goal of moving 4 meters in a straight line to collide with a target directly ahead
- Tuned gain coefficients to achieve desired effect

Tools: Arduino, Python, IMUs

**Portable Foldable Changing Table:**

- Designed portable changing table for adolescents with mobility inhibiting conditions
- Followed engineering design process all the way from brainstorming to prototyping
- Learned how to keep all of our work well documented to make collaboration easier
- Presented final prototype to panel of our client and the course professors (EDES 220)

Tools: 3D Printing, Woodwork, CAD, Prototyping

**Inverted Pendulum Simulation:**

- Used PID controls to model inverted pendulum balanced by a cart
- Calculated equations of motion using Lagrangian
- Plotted behavior in python and tested with various different masses

Tools: Python

**3D Printed Collapsable Bridge Project:**

- Designed foldable bridge for final project following design criteria
- Ran FEA in Onshape to predict behavior under center load
- Optimized bridge to maximize strength to weight ratio
- Placed in top 5 of entire class

Tools: Onshape, FEA, 3D Printing
